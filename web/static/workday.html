            How Unblock Earns Your Trust

            Responsible AI
            What the AI is doing
            Every step is logged live on Agent Activity: which agent, which check, which stop.
            Why it recommended this
            Every claim quotes an approved doc word for word, with its date and owner. Unsupported claims are blocked before you see them.
            What data it used
            Your name, role, team and office from your profile, and the question. Never private messages, reviews or pay.
            What it wants to do
            Each action shows the system, the exact form fields and the source doc before anything happens.
            Where you decide
            Approve, edit or reject every message and action. Access also needs your manager.
            What it will never do
            Touch passwords or MFA, approve its own requests, or decide pay, performance, benefits or anyone's job.
            When a person steps in
            High-impact questions stop before any search and name the right person. Gated access goes to your manager.
            When it's unsure
            Below 60/100 confidence, or when docs disagree, it doesn't guess: it finds who to ask and drafts the message.
            How to challenge it
            Say "this looks wrong" (the doc owner is asked) or "wrong person" (routing learns). Fairness rotation spreads questions across qualified people.
